# Product

The global product intent. This is the "why" of the entire project. Unlike per-work-unit `intent.md` files, this one is living — it evolves as the product evolves.

## What this is

A clear statement of what the product is. One paragraph.

## Who it's for

The target users or use case. Be specific.

## Why it exists

The problem it solves or the value it creates. What changes for the user if this product exists vs. doesn't.

## Core principles

Non-negotiable principles that guide product decisions. Examples: "privacy first", "offline-capable", "mobile-only", "self-hostable". These act as a constitution for the product.

## Out of scope

What this product explicitly is NOT. Prevents scope creep.

## Current state

A brief snapshot of where the product is in its lifecycle (prototype, MVP, production, etc.) and what the current focus is.
